A classic broadsheet front page summing up 25 November 2026 in one image:
the Houthi attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery leads, with panels
on the Cairo tanker fire, US tariffs on 14 partners, the Huawei trial
verdict, the postponed Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, the Shenzhou-24
docking, and the Wall Street slide, plus an "ALSO TODAY" ticker. The page
is drawn by `render_front_page.py` (Python + Pillow) and `main.ts` copies
the finished `entry.png` to the output directory.
